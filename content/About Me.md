## Why i started this blog 

In the world of AI, getting hold of information is easy, and i like to think of myself as a 
synthesizer: someone who takes different ideas, information, or methods and combines them into a single, unified perspective or approach. I learn from various sources, and then write it down in my own words and understanding.

A few reasons i write in public : 
- _It sharpens my thinking._ When I know someone else might read what I write, I pay more attention to detail and dig deeper into the rabbit hole, instead of settling for a surface-level understanding.
- _It might help someone else_. If my notes or explanations save someone else's time or spark an idea, that's a win.
- _It invites disagreement_. Someone might push back on what I write, and that's exactly the kind of discussion I'm looking for.

Many of these posts are living documents I come back and edit them as my understanding evolves.
